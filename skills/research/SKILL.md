# Research

Define the question and the decision it can change. Investigate only the facts needed to support that answer.

Prefer the evidence that owns the claim: official documentation/specifications, relevant source and configuration, or direct observations and measurements. Use secondary sources for discovery, then follow material claims to primary evidence. Match the actual version and environment; the latest documentation may not describe the deployed system.

Reuse sufficiently current findings already established in the session. Verify unresolved or materially changed details instead of repeating every search. Separate documented claims, observed behavior, and inference; cite material conclusions and surface consequential contradictions or gaps.

Stop when evidence supports the answer or decision, stating remaining material uncertainty. Answer in conversation by default. A durable report under `docs/pragma/` or delegation is useful only when the task's scale or future reuse justifies it.

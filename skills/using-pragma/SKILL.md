# Using Pragma

Apply relevant skills automatically, selecting them by description. Read explicitly requested and selected skills before the work they govern; reuse full bodies in context. Inspect uncertain candidates when their guidance could materially affect the work, including subtasks.

Resolve material requirements or diagnose failures before implementation; scale the process to uncertainty and risk.

After compaction, recover task state and progress; reload missing relevant guidance before continuing.

Read relevant references directly:

- [Planning](../design-grill/references/planning.md): nontrivial implementation plans.
- [Workspace isolation](../delegating/references/isolation.md): requested isolation, worktrees, or concurrent/conflicting work, including solo work.
- [Observability](../reviewing-code/references/observability.md): consequential instrumentation, logs, metrics, dashboards, or alerts.

For all code-related work, including debugging, reviews, and design decisions, **read and apply [ponytail](../ponytail/SKILL.md)**; keep its full rules active, rather than a summary.

After implementation, read and apply [reviewing-code](../reviewing-code/SKILL.md) for proportionate whole-change assessment.

For coding delegation, pass exact skill paths and relevant context; require agents to apply Ponytail and relevant task guidance.
